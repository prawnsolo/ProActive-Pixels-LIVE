## LeadForm.astro — Full Detail

**Section ID:** `#assessment`

**Quiz flow** (4 steps before form, then reveal):

| Step | ID | Question | Options (data-val) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | `#q1` | What type of organization are you? | `health`, `social`, `education`, `other` |
| Q2 | `#q2` | Where does your team lose the most time? | `intake`, `scheduling`, `reporting`, `followup` |
| Q3 | `#q3` | What is your role in decisions like this? | `ed`, `ops`, `board`, `staff` |
| Form | `#q4` | Contact fields | name, org, role, email, phone, staff_size (select) |
| Disqualifier | `#q-disqualify` | Shown if `staff_size === 'under5'` | — |
| Reveal | `#q5` | Phone number reveal + personalized message | — |

**Form submission payload** (POST to Cloudflare Worker):
```json
{
  "first_name": "...",
  "org_name": "...",
  "role": "...",
  "email": "...",
  "phone": "...",
  "staff_size": "5to15 | 15to50 | 50plus | under5",
  "niche": "[Q1 answer]",
  "biggest_drain": "[Q2 answer]",
  "contact_role": "[Q3 answer]"
}
```

**Worker endpoint:** `https://proactivepixels-router.aqua-noriega.workers.dev/submit`
- Accepts POST `application/json`
- Required fields: `first_name`, `phone`, `niche`
- Returns `{ "success": true }` on 200
- CORS locked to `https://proactivepixels.com`

**Soft disqualifier:** If `staff_size === 'under5'`, form does NOT submit to the Worker — shows `#q-disqualify` panel instead.

**Meta Pixel guard:** When Q3 is complete (fields become visible), `window.fbq` is temporarily replaced with a no-op to prevent pixel auto-event capture on form fields. Restored in `showReveal()` and the disqualifier path.

**Phone reveal:** `(540) 600-0080` — MON–FRI · 8AM–8PM ET

## Analytics / Tracking Constraints (HIPAA-Adjacent)

> **NEVER violate these rules when editing BaseLayout, LeadForm, or any analytics config:**

1. GTM fires on **anonymous page views only** — never on form interaction events
2. `dataLayer.push` is wrapped by the PII guard in BaseLayout — it strips `email`, `phone`, `first_name`, `org_name`, `name` from every push
3. `window.fbq` is suppressed when LeadForm Q4 fields are visible (restored in showReveal / disqualifier)
4. Do NOT add `fbq('track', 'Lead', {...})` with org context or any identifiable data
5. The Capacity Review form does NOT collect PHI — but org names and contact details through Meta/Google infrastructure is a trust violation in this niche
