Executive Summary
390 hours of clinical capacity. Lost every year to a problem that can be automated.

Piedmont Free Clinic sees approximately 30 patients per day. At a 7% no-show rate, that is roughly 2 missed appointments daily. Each one represents 45 minutes of clinical staff preparation time that never resulted in care.

That is 390 hours per year. Ten full work weeks. This assessment identifies appointment reminder automation as your highest-impact, lowest-risk starting point. Three additional workflows where automation can reclaim hours and redirect them to the patients on your waitlist are covered in the sections that follow.

Free clinics serving uninsured and underserved populations consistently see no-show rates above 7% due to transportation barriers, work schedule conflicts, and limited rescheduling access. Your actual figure may be higher. The automation addresses the root cause: patients who forget, not patients who cannot attend.

Recommended Solution: Priority 1

Automated appointment reminders with two-way confirmation.

Every patient scheduled receives an automated reminder 48 hours before their appointment, followed by a 24-hour confirmation request. If they confirm, the slot is locked. If they do not respond or cancel, the slot opens for waitlist backfill automatically. Your staff doesn't touch the phone.

Front desk staff stops spending 2+ hours daily on manual confirmation calls
