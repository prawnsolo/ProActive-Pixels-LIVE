The Real Cost

Every grant you wrote
funded this too.

01

Intake that takes longer than the visit

New patient onboarding averages 35 minutes of staff time before anyone sees a clinician. Insurance verification, consent forms, demographic entry across systems that don't talk to each other. Your program coordinator didn't get into this work to enter data.

02

Scheduling that still needs a phone call

Every rescheduled appointment that requires a human is a reminder not sent, a slot that sat empty, a patient who stopped trying. The front desk absorbs all of it. You absorb the gaps in your metrics.

03

Follow-up that depends on someone remembering

Most missed follow-ups aren't a staff problem. They're a systems problem. No automated trigger. No workflow. Just a person scanning a list and hoping. With PHI involved, hope is not a protocol.

04

Reports that start the week they're due

The data exists. It's spread across your EHR, intake forms, and a spreadsheet someone built in 2018. Pulling a grant deliverable takes four days and two staff members. That's not a data problem. That's an architecture problem.
